The problem

The answer exists. Nobody can find it.

Every company writes things down and then spends years answering the same questions out loud anyway. The document is in a shared drive somewhere, on page 34, under a heading nobody would think to search for.

The same questions, forever

How many leave days do I get. Who signs off on this. What is the expense limit. The policy answers all of it, and someone answers it again by hand every week.

Search only finds words

File search matches the words you typed. It does not know that "time off" and "annual leave" are the same thing, and it cannot read a scanned contract at all.

Not everything is for everyone

Salary bands and signed contracts cannot sit in the same open pile as the office wifi password — but locking them away means nobody can ask about them either.

What you get

Built for documents that matter

Not a general-purpose chatbot with your files bolted on. Every part of it assumes the answer has to be traceable and the wrong person must not see it.

Every answer names its source

Each reply says which document it came from. If the documents do not cover the question, it says so plainly instead of inventing something that sounds right.

Reads scanned paper too

A contract someone photographed and saved as a PDF is just pixels to normal search. Optical character recognition turns it back into text so it can be asked about like anything else.

Private folders stay private

Mark a folder private and name the people who may read it. For everyone else the chatbot does not merely decline to answer — it never retrieves that content in the first place.

Two roles, no surprises

Administrators upload, organise and delete. Everyone else can only ask questions. Members never see the document list, and cannot upload or remove anything.

Answers in your language

Ask in English, Vietnamese, Japanese or Spanish and the reply comes back in the language you asked — even when the document you are asking about is written in another one.

A record of what was asked

Administrators can read the full question history. It is the fastest way to find out which policy is confusing everyone, and which one needs rewriting.

How it works

Three steps, one afternoon

No integration project, no data migration, nothing to install on anyone's computer.

Upload what you already have

Drag in PDFs, Word files and plain text, up to 25 MB each. Sort them into folders the way you already think about them — HR, Finance, Contracts.

Decide who reads what

Folders are open to your whole company by default. Mark one private and list the addresses allowed in. Nested folders inherit that restriction, so a subfolder cannot quietly leak its parent.

Invite your team to ask

Send an invitation by email address. They sign up, open the chat, and start asking. There is nothing to learn — it is a message box.

Security

Your documents are not the product

The most common question we get is what happens to the files. The short version is below; the full explanation spells out where everything is stored and who can reach it.

One company cannot see another

Every search is filtered by organisation before it runs, not after. There is no query path that can return another customer's content.

Your files are not used for training

Documents are stored to answer your team's questions and nothing else. They are never used to train a model, and never shared with other customers.

Permission is checked before retrieval

The list of folders a person may read is worked out first, and only that list is searched. A private folder is invisible to the search, not filtered out of the answer afterwards.

Deleting means deleting

Remove a document and the stored file, the extracted text and the search index entries all go with it. Close your account and the same applies to everything in it.

Questions

Things worth knowing before you start

What file types can I upload?

PDF, Word (.docx) and plain text, up to 25 MB per file. Scanned PDFs work on the paid plans: the text is recognised automatically, which takes a little longer than a normal upload and counts towards your monthly page allowance.

What happens when the free trial ends?

The trial runs for three days. If you have not upgraded by then, the documents you uploaded are deleted along with their search index and chat history. Your account, your organisation and your members stay — upgrade later and you simply upload again.

Can it make things up?

It is instructed to answer only from your documents and to say so when they do not contain the answer. Because every reply names the document it drew on, you can check it. No system of this kind is infallible, which is exactly why the source is always shown.

Who can see our documents?

Administrators in your organisation see everything. Members see only what a folder's permissions allow, and never the document list itself. Our own system administrators can technically read stored content in order to operate and support the service; that access is limited to the people who need it.

What happens if two people ask the same thing?

Each gets an answer built from the documents they personally have access to. If one of them may read the private contracts folder and the other may not, their answers will legitimately differ.

Do you use our documents to train a model?

No. Your files are stored so your team can ask questions about them. They are not used for training and are not shared with anyone else.

How do I cancel?

Billing is monthly and stops when you stop paying — there is no contract term and no cancellation fee. Your plan stays active until the period you have already paid for runs out.
