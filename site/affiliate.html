Affiliate programme

20% of every monthly payment · 30% of every yearly one

If BotClarify works for your company, it probably works for companies like yours. Recommend it, and you take a share of what they pay — not once, but every time they pay, for as long as they stay a customer.

What you earn

What the customer paysYour shareExample
A monthly payment20%$79 a month → $15.80 to you, every month
A yearly payment30%$777 a year → $233.10 to you, every year

It is a share of the money actually received, so if a customer moves up a plan your commission moves with them. There is no cap and no end date: a customer who stays for four years earns you a commission in each of those four years.

Who can take part

The programme is open to BotClarify customers. You need an account and an organization on a paid plan — not because we want the revenue, but because a recommendation is worth something only when it comes from somebody who actually uses the thing. If a subscription lapses, the affiliate account pauses until it comes back; commissions already recorded are unaffected.

Join from the Affiliate tab in your console once you are on a paid plan.

How it works

  1. You get a link of your own, of the form https://botclarify.com/?ref=yourcode.
  2. Somebody follows it and creates a company account. That company is recorded as yours, permanently — they do not have to sign up the same day, and the code survives a detour through the pricing page.
  3. They pay. A commission is recorded at the rate above, against that payment.
  4. Thirty days later it becomes payable, and we send it to your PayPal address.

Why the thirty days

A payment can be refunded or charged back after it lands. The hold means we are paying out of money we have actually kept, which is the only way to run this without asking for anything back later.

Getting paid

Add a PayPal address on your affiliate page — it is the one thing we cannot do without. Payouts are made by hand, so they are not instant; you can see at any time what is held, what is ready and what has been sent. There is no minimum balance.

The rules

These exist so the programme stays worth having for everybody in it.

  • You cannot refer yourself. A company you own or administer earns nothing, on any account.
  • A company can only be claimed once. The first code wins, and an existing customer cannot be claimed by anybody.
  • No paid search on our name. Bidding on “BotClarify” and its misspellings puts you between us and people already looking for us.
  • No spam, and nothing that looks like us. No unsolicited bulk email, and no page, advertisement or account presented as though it were BotClarify.
  • No coupon, cashback or bidding sites harvesting the last click of a purchase that was already going to happen.
  • Say what you actually think. Claims about what the product does should be ones you would be happy for us to read.

Breaking these suspends the account: no further commissions are earned, though anything already payable is still paid. We would rather write to you first, and normally will.
