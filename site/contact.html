Frequently asked before buying

Can we try it with our own confidential documents?

You can, and most people do — it is the only test that means anything. Read the security page first, particularly the section on what our own staff can technically access, and decide from there.

Do you offer annual billing or an invoice?

Yes to both. Choose Yearly on the pricing page and you pay once for twelve months and keep 18%. Every payment produces a PDF invoice you can download from Plan & billing in your console, with your company name and address on it — set those in Settings first if your finance team needs them.

We need more than the Business plan allows.

Tell us the numbers — documents, people, questions per month — and we will quote for it.
